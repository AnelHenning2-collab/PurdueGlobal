**Course:** IT513 Research and Writing in IT | Purdue Global University

## Narrative Essay — iPhone Security Lesson

A first-person narrative about syncing an iPhone for a secure internet connection. The experience covers updating to the latest iOS version, creating iCloud backups, and dealing with a device freeze during a run — losing navigation.

**Security takeaways:** Creating device backups, enabling Find My Device, configuring Stolen iPhone feature, and redoing Face ID and Touch ID.

---

## Descriptive Essay — Running at Different Times of Day

A descriptive writing piece exploring the sensory experience of running at sunset, morning, and midnight. Covers safety considerations (reflective gear, spotlights), training principles (mileage building, surface selection), and the importance of warming up, hydration, and cooling down.

**Key insight:** In Florida, complete training by 9am to avoid dehydration.
