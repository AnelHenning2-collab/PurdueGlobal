## Industry Transformations

Bitcoin is known for the financial industry via decentralized digital currency. Bitcoin prevented fraud by adding traceability in Supply Chain Management. In Healthcare, Bitcoin secured patient-centric data. Bitcoin implemented smart grids and carbon tracking for Energy and Sustainability industries (Kiayias and Leonardos, 2024).

---

## Global Cryptocurrencies

- **Bitcoin (BTC):** Original cryptocurrency — decentralization, Byzantine Agreement (BA), proof-of-work, "digital gold"
- **Ethereum (ETH):** Smart contracts and dApps; PoW to PoS transition
- **Litecoin (LTC):** Faster, more scalable Bitcoin alternative
- **Monero (XMR):** Focus on privacy and financial anonymity

---

## Bitcoin and the Fourth Industrial Revolution

The volatile development of Bitcoin started during political uncertainty with Economic and Political Uncertainty (EPU). Kearney and Roy (2024) described Blockchain records as all transactions without interference from bank or government — decentralized with no central authority. There are limited 21 million bitcoins which remain anonymous and transparent with secured cryptographs.

## The Bitcoin Backbone Protocol

Garay, Kiayias, and Leonardos (2024) published the Bitcoin Backbone analysis — a decentralized cryptocurrency with Byzantine agreement (BA) and strong focus on Proof-of-Work. The backbone includes analysis and applications to ensure consistency in chain quality and chain growth property.
