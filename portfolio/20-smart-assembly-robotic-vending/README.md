# Smart Assembly Lines + Robotic Vending Machines

**Course:** Client Project — Smart Productions | Purdue Global University
**Professor:** N/A (Client Work)
**Programs:** Adobe InDesign, Adobe Photoshop

**Intent:** Convergence of product assembly and robotic vending machines in smart productions. Robots adapt to product configurations for customer custom preferences and efficiencies.

---

## Smart Assembly Lines

Imagine assembly lines with robotic arms that swiftly assemble products. These robots can adapt to different product configurations, ensuring efficient customization.

---

## On-Demand Customization

Vending machines evolve into customizable kiosks. Customers select their desired features, and the machine assembles the product on spot.

---

## Interactive Interfaces

Touchscreens on vending machines allow customers to customize orders, view assembly processes, and learn about product origins.

---

## Self-Healing Machines & Predictive Maintenance

Vending machines equipped with diagnostic sensors — when a component malfunctions, the machine self-diagnoses and orders replacement parts from the assembly line. Data analytics predict when maintenance is required, minimizing downtime.

---

## Availability & Future

The synergy between product assembly and vending machines holds immense potential. As technology advances, we'll witness a seamless blend of convenience, customization, and sustainability with these intelligent systems.
