## The Human Impact of Cryptocurrency Mining

Goodman, et al. (2020) published in Elsevier about the use of cryptocurrency mining and how timestamps are added to new blocks in chain to ensure time spent in proof-of-work. Four cryptocurrencies analyzed: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin and Monero — and how they transform technologies to generate social benefits.

The Center for Air, Climate, and Energy Solutions (CACES) measured air quality pollution associated with human mortality and climate impacts of mining cryptocurrencies in the US and China. In 2018, the results were: $1 of Bitcoin created $0.49 in health and climate changes in US and $0.37 in China.

## Blockchain in Healthcare

Blockchain technology revolutionized healthcare by providing integrity, transparency, and privacy. It enhanced data security, privacy, and internal operations with patient-based entity-based apps (Jain, et al., 2024). Providing accuracy in databases enables secure and efficient data sharing, patient consent management, oversight drug supply chain, and clinical trial management. Blockchain's decentralized nature ensures health records are not tampered with and accessible only by authorized parties.

## Humanitarian Action and Development

Zwitter and Boisse-Despiaux (2018) published "Blockchain for Humanitarian Action and Development Aid" about how blockchain revolutionized financial forecasting with smart contracts. In 2018, Bitcoin was remembered as a database that provides aid to humanitarian crisis areas. During the Blockchain4SDGs Workshop panelists discussed blockchain development for international peace, security, new technology, and humanitarian action.

Blockchain characterized itself as a Digital Ledger Technology (DLT) which is censorship proof, reliable, pseudo anonymous, secure, resilient, consistent, and upstanding.

## Bitcoin Post-Election 2024

Key milestones:
- **2018:** Bitcoin's first bubble at $19,500
- **Feb 2021:** Tesla bought $1.5B in bitcoin
- **2022:** FTX collapsed; Bitcoin dropped from $60K to $20K
- **Late 2023:** BlackRock ETF launch; Bitcoin climbed to $30K
- **Jan 2024:** SEC approved bitcoin ETF at $40K
- **Nov 6, 2024:** Trump won presidential election; Bitcoin at $78K
- **Dec 5, 2024:** Bitcoin hit $100,710

## How AI Affected the Crypto Market

Singh, et al. (2024) wrote in Reuters about "Cryptoverse: AI tokens outpace record-breaking bitcoin." Coins linked to AI-focused crypto projects with Nvidia created the biggest spike in cryptocurrency history — CoinGecko data's combined value reached $26.4 billion vs $2.7 billion in April 2023.

CoinDesk Indices Computer Index outgrew Bitcoin by 15% (165% vs 151% in twelve months).
